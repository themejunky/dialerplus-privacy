Dialer Plus – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Dialer Plus (package com.dialerplus.app) is a phone app for Android published by ThemeJunky: a dialer with contacts, call history, an in-call screen and themes. It can be set as your default phone app. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data. See also the Terms of Use.

Contacts, call history and calls stay on your device

To work as a phone app, Dialer Plus reads your contacts (names, numbers, photos, starred contacts) and your call history, and it writes to them only when you act: starring a contact, deleting or clearing call history, blocking a number. Calls are placed, answered and managed through Android's own telephony services. All of this happens on the device only. We never upload your contacts, phone numbers, call history, contact photos or search text, and we never record call audio. Blocked numbers are stored in Android's own blocked-numbers list.

Decline with message

When you decline a call with a quick reply, the app asks for the optional SMS permission the first time. If you grant it, the reply is sent directly as a text message (standard carrier charges may apply). If you decline the permission, your SMS app opens with the reply prepared, and you decide whether to send it. Dialer Plus does not read or store your text messages.

Themes and settings

Your chosen theme, unlocked themes, dialpad preferences (tones, vibration), quick replies and other settings are saved on the device and deleted when you uninstall the app. Themes are bundled with the app; no theme request contains personal data.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob and its mediation partners. Ad networks may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interactions, as described in the Google advertising policy and partner policies. Where required, a consent form is shown before ads are requested, and you can change your choice in the app settings. Ads are never shown during a call or on the incoming-call screen. Premium removes ads.

Analytics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config) to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. The data is limited to anonymous usage events (for example: setup completed, theme applied, call placed or answered), device model, Android version, app version, country and language, and crash stack traces. Analytics is configured without the advertising ID. We do not link this data to your name, phone number or contacts, and we do not sell it. Google's processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security.

Purchases

Dialer Plus Premium is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase, through Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details. Purchase status is checked on the device through Google Play; purchase tokens are not sent to us.

Permissions

Phone, call log, contacts, answer calls and read phone numbers/state: the core functions of a default phone app (dialing, in-call screen, call history, SIM selection). Send SMS (optional): decline-with-message replies. Notifications and full-screen notifications: incoming and ongoing call screens. Vibrate: dialpad feedback. Internet and network state: ads, Firebase and Google Play Billing. Bluetooth connect: routing call audio to a headset.

Dialer Plus Themes (theme catalog app)

Dialer Plus Themes (package com.dialerplus.theme.catalog) is a companion app that only carries theme collections for Dialer Plus. It has no permissions, reads none of your contacts, calls or messages, and only hands the selected theme to Dialer Plus on your device. It shows a small banner ad through Google AdMob and uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for anonymous usage and crash statistics; everything in the Advertising and Analytics sections above applies to it in the same way. Uninstalling it removes its themes from Dialer Plus.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Samsung Galaxy Store and Huawei AppGallery

This section applies to com.dialerplus.app.samsung on Samsung Galaxy Store and com.dialerplus.app.huawei on Huawei AppGallery. For these Android editions, it takes precedence over store-specific statements elsewhere on this page. Dialer Plus Themes uses com.dialerplus.theme.catalog.samsung and com.dialerplus.theme.catalog.huawei, respectively.

Purchases. Galaxy Store builds use Samsung In-App Purchase (Samsung Electronics); AppGallery builds use HUAWEI IAP (Huawei/Aspiegel, depending on your region). Google Play Billing is not used. The app receives product IDs, purchase tokens or identifiers, receipts/signatures and purchase status to verify ownership, unlock Premium and restore purchases. The store handles payment details; we do not receive your card details. See the Samsung Privacy Policy and Huawei Privacy Statement and the notices shown by your store. Manage purchases and any subscriptions in the store where you bought them; uninstalling does not cancel subscriptions. Dialer Plus Themes has no purchases; this paragraph applies only to the Dialer Plus phone app.

Ads. Galaxy Store builds use Google AdMob and Google UMP consent. The Samsung phone app also includes Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) mediation; Dialer Plus Themes does not. AppGallery builds use AdMob with UMP when Google Play services are present, and Huawei Petal Ads with Huawei consent otherwise. Providers may process the Android advertising ID (AdMob), OAID (Petal), IP address, device information, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Use the app privacy/consent options where available, Android Settings → Privacy → Ads to reset/delete the advertising ID, or Huawei Settings → Privacy → Ads and privacy to reset OAID or limit personalized ads (names vary by device). See Google’s Privacy Policy, Huawei’s Privacy Statement and Liftoff’s Privacy Policy. The AppGallery phone app also packages the Liftoff AdMob adapter; the no-Google-services route uses Petal Ads. Meta Audience Network is not included in these OEM builds.

Analytics and push. On Galaxy Store and AppGallery builds, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Performance Monitoring, Cloud Messaging and App Check are not used. The Firebase analytics, crash-reporting and push descriptions elsewhere on this page do not apply to these editions.

Permissions and online services. Dialer Plus uses phone, contacts and call-log permissions for its default-phone features and optional SMS permission for replies you choose to send. Contacts, call history and message replies are not uploaded; call audio is not recorded. Dialer Plus Themes reads no contacts, calls or messages and passes selected themes to the matching store edition of Dialer Plus. Theme catalog/download requests remain in use; servers receive IP address and normal request data.

Support messages (Dialer Plus Themes). If you choose to contact us from inside the Galaxy Store or AppGallery edition of Dialer Plus Themes, nothing is sent until you tap Send. The request then contains your message, the email address you optionally enter, a random install ID, the app package, app version and selected theme, your Android version, device model and language. We use it only to answer and troubleshoot your request; it is not used for advertising and is not shared with ad providers. Support messages are kept to handle your request and its follow-ups; you can ask us at any time to delete them by writing to the contact address below.
