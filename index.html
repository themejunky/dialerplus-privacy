Dialer Plus – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Dialer Plus (package com.dialerplus.app) is a phone app for Android published by ThemeJunky: a dialer with contacts, call history, an in-call screen and themes. It can be set as your default phone app. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data. See also the Terms of Use.

Contacts, call history and calls stay on your device

To work as a phone app, Dialer Plus reads your contacts (names, numbers, photos, starred contacts) and your call history, and it writes to them only when you act: starring a contact, deleting or clearing call history, blocking a number. Calls are placed, answered and managed through Android's own telephony services. All of this happens on the device only. We never upload your contacts, phone numbers, call history, contact photos or search text, and we never record call audio. Blocked numbers are stored in Android's own blocked-numbers list.

Decline with message

When you decline a call with a quick reply, the app asks for the optional SMS permission the first time. If you grant it, the reply is sent directly as a text message (standard carrier charges may apply). If you decline the permission, your SMS app opens with the reply prepared, and you decide whether to send it. Dialer Plus does not read or store your text messages.

Themes and settings

Your chosen theme, unlocked themes, dialpad preferences (tones, vibration), quick replies and other settings are saved on the device and deleted when you uninstall the app. Themes are bundled with the app; no theme request contains personal data.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob and its mediation partners. Ad networks may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interactions, as described in the Google advertising policy and partner policies. Where required, a consent form is shown before ads are requested, and you can change your choice in the app settings. Ads are never shown during a call or on the incoming-call screen. Premium removes ads.

Analytics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config) to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. The data is limited to anonymous usage events (for example: setup completed, theme applied, call placed or answered), device model, Android version, app version, country and language, and crash stack traces. Analytics is configured without the advertising ID. We do not link this data to your name, phone number or contacts, and we do not sell it. Google's processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security.

Purchases

Dialer Plus Premium is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase, through Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details. Purchase status is checked on the device through Google Play; purchase tokens are not sent to us.

Permissions

Phone, call log, contacts, answer calls and read phone numbers/state: the core functions of a default phone app (dialing, in-call screen, call history, SIM selection). Send SMS (optional): decline-with-message replies. Notifications and full-screen notifications: incoming and ongoing call screens. Vibrate: dialpad feedback. Internet and network state: ads, Firebase and Google Play Billing. Bluetooth connect: routing call audio to a headset.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.
